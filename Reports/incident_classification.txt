========================================
     SOC INCIDENT CLASSIFICATION
========================================

Incident ID: SOC-2026-001

Incident Type:
Suspected Phishing Email

Source:
Email Security Investigation

Severity:
MEDIUM (Training Classification)

Investigation Findings:

1. Suspicious sender domain identified.
2. SPF authentication failed.
3. DKIM signature was absent.
4. DMARC authentication failed.
5. Suspicious URL identified.
6. Sender and Return-Path differed.

Potential Risks:

- Credential theft
- Account compromise
- Unauthorised access
- Financial fraud

Recommended Actions:

1. Quarantine the suspicious email.
2. Identify other recipients.
3. Search for similar messages.
4. Block confirmed malicious indicators.
5. Investigate affected user accounts.
6. Escalate confirmed compromise to SOC L2.

Final Classification:

SIMULATED PHISHING ATTEMPT

Investigation Status:
Completed - Training Environment
